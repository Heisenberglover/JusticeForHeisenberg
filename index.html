About Elite Horse Training

Elite Horse Training is a for-profit colt training business based in Riverton, Wyoming. We specialize in starting young horses with patience-based and ethical training methods, focusing on groundwork, calm handling, and progressive riding.

Vision

To cultivate a community where every horse and rider shares a foundation of mutual trust, creating a legacy of excellence and safety in the Wyoming equine industry.

Mission

Our mission is to train colts with patience, fairness, and clarity, creating confident, willing horses while running a successful and ethical training business.

Our Approach

We focus on groundwork, desensitization, first saddling, and gradual riding progression to build confidence and trust in each horse. We train client-owned horses and also purchase, train, and resell trained colts.

LLC Formation & Legal Structure

Services

Professional training programs designed to create calm, confident, and safe horses.

Groundwork & Desensitization

Teaching horses to respond calmly to pressure, handling, and new environments.

We build a foundation of trust through consistent, fair cues and clear boundaries. This includes leading, backing, and desensitization to common stimuli.

Colt Starting

Gentle, step-by-step introduction to saddling, riding, and real-world exposure.

We introduce tack gradually, ensure the horse understands pressure, and build confidence before the first ride.

Behavior Fixes

Addressing root causes of bad habits through trust-based training, not punishment.

Our process includes behavior assessment, identifying triggers, and retraining through structured progressions.

Versatility Training

Preparing horses for trail, pleasure, and everyday riding through consistent progression.

We build endurance, responsiveness, and reliability across multiple riding environments.
